Privacy Policy for Resume Roast

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Resume Roast ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use the Resume Roast mobile application (the "App"), which provides an AI-generated review and optional rewrite of a resume/CV you submit.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Resume content: the text you paste, or the text extracted on your device from a PDF you choose to upload. This content may include personal information you decide to include (such as your name, contact details, and work history). You control what you submit.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Language preference: your device language, used to choose the language of the review.
  • Technical data: standard information needed to deliver a network request (such as a temporary IP address handled by our hosting provider). We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

  • In-app purchases (credit packs) are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a confirmation that a purchase occurred; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

How Your Resume Is Processed

When you request a review or a rewrite, the resume text is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our backend (Google Cloud / Firebase Functions), which forwards it to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the result that is returned to your device.

  • We do not store your resume text on our servers. It is processed transiently to produce the result and is not retained by us afterwards.
  • Our AI provider processes the text to generate the output under its API terms. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models by default.

Data Stored On Your Device

The following are stored locally on your device only, never on our servers:

  • Your history of past results (score, verdict, and breakdown).
  • Your remaining credits and app preferences.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all of this local information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate the AI review and optional rewrite of your resume.
  • To display and store your results locally so you can revisit them.
  • To manage your credit balance and process purchases.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Cloud / Firebase Functions — hosts our backend that processes requests.
  • Anthropic — provides the AI model that generates the review/rewrite from the submitted text.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases.

Data Security

  • Resume text is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • We do not retain resume text on our servers after generating a result.
  • The AI provider key and processing happen server-side; the App never contacts the AI vendor directly.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

  • Access: your results and credits are visible within the App.
  • Deletion: clear local data by using "Clear history" in the App, or by clearing the App's data / uninstalling it. Because we do not retain your resume text, there is no server-side copy to delete.
  • Questions: contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
