Privacy Policy for Daily Quest - Habit Tracker RPG

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Daily Quest ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Daily Quest - Habit Tracker RPG (the "App").

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Habit Data: Names and descriptions of habits you create
  • Progress Data: Completion status, streaks, experience points, and achievements
  • User Preferences: Theme settings, notification preferences

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Usage Data: App performance data, crash reports (if applicable)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Track your habit progress and achievements
  • Calculate streaks and experience points
  • Display your analytics and insights
  • Save your preferences and settings

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not:

  • Upload your personal data to external servers
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use cloud storage for your habit information

Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data:

  • All data remains on your device
  • We implement standard security measures to protect data integrity
  • We do not transmit personal data over the internet

Children's Privacy

Our App does not specifically target children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App can be used by people of all ages as it promotes healthy habit formation.

Third-Party Services

Our App currently does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. If this changes in future updates, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data (available within the App)
  • Delete your data (by uninstalling the App or clearing app data)
  • Modify your habits and preferences at any time

Data Deletion

To delete all your data:

  1. Go to your device's Settings
  2. Navigate to Apps/Application Manager
  3. Find "Daily Quest"
  4. Select "Clear Data" or uninstall the App

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
